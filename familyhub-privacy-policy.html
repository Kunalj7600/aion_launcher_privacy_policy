What we store

Hub keeps your family’s content online so it can sync between phones. It uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to do this. We only store what you put into the app, plus a random account ID.

WhatWhy AION Hub uses it
Anonymous account IDCreated automatically when you open the app. It is a random ID, not tied to your name, email or phone number.
Display name and avatarShown to the people in your family group.
Family group detailsGroup name, invite codes and who belongs to the group.
StatusThe status you pick, such as “At home”, or the words you type. It also appears on linked AION Launchers.
Notes and to-dosThe text you write, with your name and avatar attached.
PhotosPictures you share. Hub shrinks them to at most 1080 pixels and stores a thumbnail plus the full image.
Voice notesAudio you record in the app and send to the group.
Snaps and pokesView-once photos and short nudges, with who sent them and when.
Launcher link codeA short code that connects your AION Launcher to your Hub profile.
Settings on your phoneYour notification switch and performance mode. These stay on the device.

Firebase is operated by Google. Data held there is subject to Firebase’s privacy information as well as this policy. Data is encrypted in transit. It is not end-to-end encrypted, so it is protected by server access rules rather than keys only your family holds.

Permissions AION Hub may ask for

Each permission is tied to one feature. If you decline it, that feature stops working and the rest of the app carries on.

Microphone

Used only to record voice notes while you are recording one. Recordings are sent to your family group.

Camera and photos

Hub opens the system camera or Android’s photo picker, so it only receives the image you choose. Taking a snap uses the camera the same way.

Notifications

Lets Hub tell you about new snaps, notes, voice notes, to-dos and pokes while the app is in the background. You can switch these off from the menu inside the app.

Storage (Android 9 and older)

Only used to save a shared photo to your gallery when you tap download.

Internet

Needed to sync your family’s content.

Who can see it

  • Your family group sees your name, avatar, status, notes, to-dos, photos and voice notes.
  • Snaps and pokes can only be read by the sender and the receiver. A snap is deleted from the server the moment the receiver opens it.
  • A linked AION Launcher can show pokes, snaps and group content for its owner, and can upload photos for them. You can unlink it at any time.
  • Service providers that run Firebase process the data on our behalf.

Anyone in your group can screenshot a snap or save a shared photo, so share with people you trust.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or work with ad networks, and the app has no analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don’t sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don’t read your location. The “Where is everyone?” button only sends a message asking where someone is.
  • We don’t access your contacts, call history, SMS or other files.
  • We don’t use your content to build profiles.

Delete your data

  • Delete your own notes, to-dos, photos and voice notes inside the app. The group owner can delete any of them.
  • Leave a family from the app menu to be removed from its member list.
  • Uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove what is saved on your phone.
  • To erase your profile and everything linked to your account ID, email us with the subject “Delete my AION Hub data” and include your display name and family group name. We confirm by reply and finish within 30 days.
  • Group owners can ask for the whole group and its content to be deleted in the same email.

Children

AION Hub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has a profile, contact us and we will delete it. Parents and guardians who set Hub up for a child are responsible for what that child shares in the family.

Changes to this policy

If AION Hub’s behavior changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
