What stays on your device

AION is a home screen. To work, it needs to know a few things about your phone. All of the following is processed on the device and is not uploaded.

WhatWhy AION uses it
Installed apps and iconsBuilds the app drawer and your tiles, and refreshes them when you install, update or remove an app.
Notifications and media playbackPowers the music tile and live notification tiles. Only available after you turn on Notification access in Android settings.
Photos you pickUsed for photo tiles and wallpapers. AION copies the photos you choose into its private storage, and deletes that copy when you remove the photo from a tile.
Battery level and dateDrawn on the battery and calendar tiles. The calendar tile shows the date only. It does not read your calendar events.
Android widgets you addAION hosts widgets such as clocks or weather. Each widget belongs to its own app and follows that app’s privacy policy.
Your layout and settingsTile names, sizes, positions and preferences are saved in AION’s private app storage.

Permissions AION may ask for

Each permission is tied to one feature. If you decline it, that feature stops working and the rest of the launcher carries on.

Notification access

Lets AION read the notifications in your shade and the current media session, so the music and notification tiles can show them. This content is used to draw tiles on your screen. It is not sent to us or to any server.

Post notifications

Lets AION show a notification when family members add something in Family sync. You can switch these alerts off inside AION.

Photo picker and wallpaper

AION opens Android’s photo picker so you choose exactly which images it can see. If you set a system wallpaper, AION asks Android to apply the image you selected.

Widget binding

Android asks for your approval each time AION hosts a widget from another app.

Family sync

Family sync is an optional feature that connects AION to the companion AION Hub app, so people in your household can share photos, statuses and short messages. It uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to do this.

What happens even if you never link

When AION starts, it connects to Firebase and signs in anonymously. This creates a random ID that is not tied to your name, email address or phone number. AION uses it so that linking is ready when you want it. Nothing else is shared until you create a link code.

What is stored once you link

  • The random link code and the anonymous ID that goes with it.
  • The display name, avatar and status text you set for yourself in the family.
  • Photos you add to the family. AION shrinks them to at most 1080 pixels and compresses them before upload, and stores both a thumbnail and the full image.
  • Pokes, and view-once photos (“snaps”) you send. A snap is deleted from the server the moment the recipient opens it.
  • Notes, voice notes and to-dos that family members create in the Hub. AION reads who added them so it can notify you.

Who can see it

People who are linked to the same family, and the service providers that run Firebase. We do not use this content for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Leaving a family

Choosing unlink in AION removes this device’s link from the family and clears the linking details saved on your phone. Content already shared with the family, such as a photo, may remain in the family space until it is removed from the Hub.

Firebase is operated by Google. Data held there is subject to Firebase’s privacy information as well as this policy.

Family sync is powered by the companion AION Hub app, which has its own policy: AION Hub privacy policy.

Search suggestions

When you type in AION’s search bar, the text you type is sent to Google’s search suggestion service so AION can show completions. Google receives that text and your device’s IP address, as with any web request.

When you tap a suggestion, AION opens google.com in your browser with that search. From that point Google’s own privacy policy applies. AION does not keep a history of your searches.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t show ads or work with ad networks.
  • We don’t build advertising profiles or sell, rent or trade your information.
  • We don’t collect your location, contacts, call history or messages.
  • We don’t ask you to create an account, or for your name, email or phone number.
  • We don’t upload your installed app list, notifications or music activity.

Your choices

  • Turn off Notification access any time in Android settings. The music and notification tiles will stop.
  • Turn off Family alerts inside AION, or unlink from your family.
  • Remove photos from tiles to delete AION’s private copy.
  • Clear AION’s storage or uninstall the launcher to remove everything saved on your phone.
  • To ask about data held in Family sync, or to have it removed, contact us using the details below. Please include your link code or family details so we can find it.

Children

AION is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Family sync is designed for people who already know each other, such as a household. Parents and guardians who set it up for a child are responsible for what that child shares in the family.

Changes to this policy

If AION’s behavior changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this page and the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.
